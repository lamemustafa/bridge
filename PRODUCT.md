# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Accountants and chartered-accountancy teams using Tally on a desktop during day-to-day receivables and payables work. They need to see which parties carry the largest exposure and how old the underlying bills are without first interpreting Bridge's sync machinery.

## Product Purpose

For Unit A, Bridge reads one verified Tally company and presents an in-memory outstandings view: receivable and payable totals, four ageing buckets, top parties, oldest-bill age, and a clear freshness line. Success means the numbers are correct, locally computed from vouchers and bill allocations, and visibly tied to a complete or explicitly partial live read.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, restrained, evidence-led.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS dashboard made from interchangeable metric cards.
- A technical capability passport, sync diagram, or evidence matrix competing with the business result.
- Decorative finance styling, unexplained color, or animation that makes the numbers feel less auditable.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the accountant's business question, not Bridge's implementation.
- Make freshness and incomplete-read states impossible to miss.
- Use familiar accounting labels and Indian-number formatting.
- Keep the first screen focused: no filters, drill-down, or configurability in Unit A.
- Prefer dense, legible comparison over decorative containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Never communicate receivable/payable or complete/partial state by color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all financial values readable at desktop and narrow-window widths.
